Aurum-Clipper uses Google's Gemini AI to analyze every part of your video. It looks at the words being spoken, the flow of the conversation, and the emotional tone. Then it gives each moment a "virality score" - a number that tells you how likely that clip is to perform well on social media.

The tool uses OpenAI's Whisper technology to transcribe your video's audio. The best part? This happens on your own computer - no cloud, no uploads, no waiting. Your videos stay private.

It automatically creates animated subtitles that pop, slide, and highlight words as they're spoken. These are the same style of subtitles you see on viral TikTok videos - the ones that keep viewers hooked even with the sound off.

### ⚡ One-Click Export
Each selected clip is exported as a standalone video file, ready to upload. No additional software needed.
